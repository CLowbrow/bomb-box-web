Quick guide

How to play

  • Move one space at a time. Push a single box or barrel; you cannot pull.
  • You can step onto boxes, but not jump up or down ledges. Use ramps to change height.
  • Hold every switch of a color to open doors of that color.
  • A barrel arms when it falls, then explodes in a plus shape. Keep your distance.
  • Reach an exit to win. If a move goes wrong, rewind one completed turn.
